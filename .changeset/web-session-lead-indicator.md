web: The session row's whole state now rides one indicator in the leading slot the sidebar already had — running, pinned, pin colour and unread — instead of a separate pin column that pushed every title to the right. A running session shows a spinner in its own pin colour (or a neutral one when unpinned), and an unread result sits as a corner badge punched out of the dot, so the seven pin colours and both themes stay legible. New pins pick a random preset colour, so pinning a few sessions in a row leaves them distinguishable at a glance rather than a column of identical dots.
